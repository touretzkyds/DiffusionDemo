About

This tab demonstrates how content filtering works in diffusion models. The model analyzes generated images to detect specific concepts that may be filtered based on a threshold value. Images are generated using your prompt, then analyzed for similarity to the filter concept you specify. Images with a similarity score above the threshold are marked as filtered.

Additional Controls:

Filter Concept:

The concept to filter for in generated images. For example, entering "cat" will detect and filter images containing cats if they score above the threshold.

Filter Threshold:

Sets the sensitivity of the filter.
